Punnari – Privacy Policy

Updated October 28, 2026

Punnari is an app for tracking your weight and body measurements. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

In short

  • All your data is stored only on your own phone.
  • The app does not send any data to the developer or to any server.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no user account.

Data the app handles

  • The measurements you log: weight, body measurements (e.g. waist) and body fat percentage, with their dates and times.
  • Your settings, such as height, goal weight, units, language and appearance.

The data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings. You can delete individual entries in the app.

Health Connect

If you turn on Health Connect, the app asks for your permission to access:

  • Weight and body fat – read and write.
  • Historical data – reading weights older than 30 days, so earlier weigh-ins can be imported at once.

Data read from Health Connect (for example weigh-ins from a smart scale) is used only to show your entries, charts and progress in the app. The app writes to Health Connect only the weights and body fat percentages you add yourself, so they also appear in your other health apps. Data from Health Connect is never transferred to others, sold or used for advertising. You can revoke the permissions at any time in Health Connect settings.

The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Backup and sharing

A CSV backup is saved only to the location you choose, and a progress image is shared only to the app you choose. Both happen only when you choose to do so. After that, the file is covered by the policies of the service where you save or share it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
